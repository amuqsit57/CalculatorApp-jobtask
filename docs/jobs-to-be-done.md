# Jobs To Be Done

Why they use it. These jobs exist whether or not this app does.

## J-1 (First-time Car Buyer)

When I am deciding whether I can afford a car I would need to borrow for, I want to know the full cost including the interest, so I can commit to a purchase without overstretching my money.

## J-2 (First-time Car Buyer)

When I am offered a lower monthly cost in exchange for borrowing for longer, I want to understand what that trade really costs me, so I can choose a repayment period I will not regret.

## J-3 (First-time Car Buyer)

When my monthly budget could be higher or lower than what a lender is asking for, I want to know how that changes when I will be debt-free and what I will pay overall, so I can plan my repayments with no surprises.

## J-4 (First-time Car Buyer)

When a lender quotes me an interest rate, I want to understand how the interest builds up on what I owe, so I can judge the offer and not take the quoted figure on trust.
