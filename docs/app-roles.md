# App Roles

Who uses the app. The app has one role, because the need it serves belongs to one kind of person.

## First-time Car Buyer

A First-time Car Buyer is a person about to finance a car for the first time, who has a rough idea of the price and a monthly budget but has never had to weigh up a loan's rate, length and total cost. They are often deciding quickly, with a dealer or lender quoting them a monthly figure.

They can enter the car price, down payment, interest rate, loan length and how the interest compounds. They can then see the monthly payment and total cost, compare different loan lengths, try paying more or less each month, and see how the interest adds up year by year.

They must never be shown a payoff time or a total cost for a monthly payment that cannot repay the loan.
